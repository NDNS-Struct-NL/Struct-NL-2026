Directions
   - Option 1: Enter on the ground floor, near the restaurant, walk all the way to the end of the corridor and take the elevator to the 4th floor.
   - Option 2: Proceed to the 1st floor, then walk to the far end and take the stairs up to the 4th floor.

The space has an informal atmosphere with tables and chairs and even couches, a coffee machine.
chalk boards and a large television screen/digiboard.
